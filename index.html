An iPhone will not let an app like this buzz — it never could, so the buzz you may have expected was never happening. A short sound is the way to know a door was marked without looking at the screen. Worth having with the phone in a mount.
Two things to know. On a recent iPhone the ring/silent switch on the side silences it, the same as it does a text — so on silent you may hear nothing at all. And it asks to play alongside your music rather than stop it. Try it in the truck with a podcast on before you rely on it.
Lots the county flags as rentals, or where the owner's post goes somewhere else, get a soft yellow wash on both maps. In some neighbourhoods that is half the street. Tap to go faint → bold → off.
No doors marked and no real movement for half an hour, and the app closes the session and stops the GPS on its own — so it is not running all evening for nothing. Nothing is lost: every door and every step is already saved. It then asks whether to carry that session on, start a fresh one, or end the day — straight away if the app is in front of you, otherwise the next time you pick the phone up. It will keep asking until you answer. If the session started on an earlier day it does not offer to carry it on, it just tells you when it closed. Either way the day writes down both times, so a short day always says why.
Two other things happen on their own and cannot be switched off: getting home ends the session, and while you are driving the breadcrumbs are spaced further apart. Both only save battery. Neither loses anything you have logged — the driving still shows on the map and still counts towards the miles driven.

Paste a Google Solar key here and a Measure the roof button appears on every house you open. It measures the roof off aerial photography — size and slope — in about a second.
What it costs you: nothing at your volume. The first 10,000 lookups a month are free and twenty a day is about 440. Google still needs a card on the account before the free allowance works, so set a daily cap of 200 in the console first and a runaway can never cost more than pocket change.
Two things to know. Around Columbus the photography is 10 cm and the numbers have been checked against your own takeoffs to about 4%. Outside the metro — Marysville, London, Newark, Lancaster, Circleville and everywhere rural — it drops to 25 cm photography, which has never been checked against a real takeoff. The panel tells you which one you got, every time.
