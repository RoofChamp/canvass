Microphone test

Takes about five minutes, not thirty seconds. Do it sat in the truck, engine off is fine, with your music or a podcast already playing through the truck speakers.

Nothing here is saved and nothing here touches the canvassing app. It only shows you what your phone actually does. Answer the yes/no questions as you go — those are the answer, not the numbers.

Test 1

Does it record at all?

Tap Start, say “testing one two three”, tap Stop, then play it back.

Not run yet.
Play it. Can you hear your own voice?
Test 2

Does your music survive it?

Start your podcast or music on the Bluetooth first. Then tap this. It opens the microphone for five seconds and closes it again — exactly what a TALK button would do.

Not run yet.
1. Did the music keep playing the whole time?
2. Is it back on the TRUCK speakers now?
Test 3

Does the fourth one still work?

Opens and closes the microphone four times, recording a short clip each time. Say something different each round — “one”, “two”, “three”, “four”.

Then play all four back yourself, using the four players that appear. On some iPhones the first is fine and the later ones come back silent, at a normal file size, with nothing on screen to say so.

Not run yet.
Play all four. Can you hear your voice in every one?
Test 4

What happens if you get interrupted?

This is the one that decides it.

Tap Start. Say “before”. Then swipe up to the home screen and wait ten seconds (or let the screen go dark). Come back here, say “after”, and tap Stop.

Not run yet.
Play it. Can you hear the word “before”?
